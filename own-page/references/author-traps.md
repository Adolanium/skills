# Traps when the page is yours

Open this when you are stuck being kind to your own draft. Calls live in `SKILL.md`.

| Trap | What you tell yourself | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Best case as the pitch | "Someone did hit 70%, so we can say it." | Say the usual case, or say "up to" and who gets it. If you cannot, cut the number. |
| We know it's true | "I ran this last month." | Put the link or the date on the page, or the reader Waits. |
| Logo as proof | "Stripe uses us, so the product works." | Keep the logo if it is true. Do not let it carry a claim the logo does not prove. |
| Coming soon as now | "It ships next week." | Date it, or move it off the ask. |
| One happy user | "The quote is real." | Fine as a quote. Do not let it stand in for a rate. |
| Bigger after review | "Let's make the claim sharper." | If the reader already Takes, stop. Do not grow the claim. |

If the only source is you, write "we say" on the page or drop the ask.
