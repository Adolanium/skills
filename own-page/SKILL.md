# Own page

You wrote the page. A careful reader will not grade every line. They will look for the one claim that has to be true or they will leave. Find that claim. Fix it, or mark it, before you publish.

This is not a rewrite of the whole page. It is not a sales pass.

## When to Use

- User says "check my copy", "before I publish", "what would hinge walk on", "stress test this landing page / README / ad."
- User pasted their own draft, URL, or screenshot and they are the author.
- User wants to know if their own claim can survive a cheap check.

Don't use for:

- A page someone else wrote that they are deciding to buy, share, or sign. Use `hinge` for that.
- Wide brand work with no page in front of you.
- Legal sign-off. A person owns that.

## Need first

- The draft, URL, or file. If they gave a URL, open it.
- A way to open the record a reader would use to break the claim. If you cannot open it, say so. Do not publish a "we're fine" note from memory.

## Steps

### 1. Name the move you are selling

One short phrase: buy this, sign up, star the repo, donate, believe this number, hire us. If you cannot name it, ask. Do not check a page with no ask.

### 2. Sit in the reader's seat

Write one sentence: "I would not do this if ___." That blank is the hinge you are leaning on, even if you never said it out loud.

Drop timers, logo walls, "trusted by", and nice adjectives. Keep the claim that, if false, makes the ask a bad idea.

At most two hinges.

### 3. Do the cheap check a stranger would do

Open the record that would get you in trouble if the number is wrong: your own docs, a filing, a spec, a test, the results table. Quote it.

If `hinge` is in this collection, you may open `../hinge/references/hides.md` for how a hinge hides. Do not copy that file here.

**Hard rule.** You may not say the page is safe unless you opened a record this turn and quoted it. Memory of your own product is not enough.

### 4. Call it as the reader

| Call | When |
|---|---|
| They Take | The hinge holds in the scope you asked for. |
| They Wait | A stranger cannot settle it from what you published plus a cheap public record. |
| They Walk | The usual reading fails, or the move is costly and you gave them nothing they can check. |

"Up to" used as the usual result is a Walk or a Wait, same as on someone else's page.

### 5. Change the page, or say what to change

Use `templates/report.md`.

- If they Walk: cut the claim, add the missing number, or add the link to the record. Propose the exact new sentence.
- If they Wait: add the cheap check to the page (link, definition, usual case), or drop the ask.
- If they Take: leave the hinge. You may still cut extra words, but do not "improve" a fine claim into a bigger one.

Do not rewrite the whole page unless they asked.

## Watch for

- Editing tone and skipping the hinge.
- Adding more praise so it "sounds safer."
- Checking a claim the reader does not need for the ask.
- Treating your own memory as the record.

## You're done when

- The move and one or two hinges are named.
- The reader-call cites a record you opened, or it is Wait because you could not.
- The user has either a new sentence to paste, or a clear "this hinge holds."
